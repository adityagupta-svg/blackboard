Our story

Decades in and still building what's next.

Blackboard has spent decades at the center of teaching and learning technology — not as a single product, but as a platform other companies build on. The Integration Partnership Program formalizes that relationship: a documented API surface, a supported developer network, and a clear commercial path from a free evaluation account to a negotiated enterprise partnership.

Partners don't bolt on to Blackboard as an afterthought. The LMS, accessibility tooling, and institutional effectiveness products were each built with integration points as first-class citizens, so what you build reaches instructors and learners inside the workflow they already use every day.

What you can build on

Each tool built around the learner.

Three product lines, three different kinds of integration surface — pick the one that matches what your product does.

Blackboard LMS

The core learning environment — course content, assessment, grading, and communication. Most integration partners start here, connecting content libraries, proctoring tools, and analytics into the course workflow.

Blackboard Ally

Accessibility built into the course-building process itself. Partners extend Ally's alternative-format pipeline and institutional accessibility scoring for their own content and compliance tooling.

Institutional Effectiveness

Accreditation, planning, program review, and outcomes reporting for the institution as a whole. Integration partners here typically connect data warehouses, SIS platforms, and reporting tools.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Submit the "Become a partner" form and tell us which tier fits your plans. Community tier accounts are approved at no cost; Developer Network purchases are processed immediately. Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers start with a conversation with our partnerships team.

Community is free and caps at 25 production implementations with limited email support. Developer Network ($3,000/yr, or $499/yr for academic and non-profit organizations) raises the cap to 50, adds a dedicated SaaS Starter Site option, and includes roughly 48-hour support response through our Support Portal.

Community and Developer Network both include access to shared SaaS test sites. If you need an isolated environment, Developer Network partners can add a dedicated SaaS Starter Site for $2,000/yr. Bronze, Silver, and Gold partnerships include a dedicated Starter Site as standard, with more available on request.

Paid tiers run 12 months from your purchase date. As renewal approaches, your partnerships contact reaches out with the renewal terms; once accepted, payment can be completed securely by card. You'll be notified well before anything lapses.

Yes. Most partners start on Community or Developer Network and move up as their implementation count and support needs grow. Bronze, Silver, and Gold are negotiated directly with our partnerships team once you outgrow Developer Network's cap.
